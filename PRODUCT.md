# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
- Primary: owners of small Thai service businesses of every kind (hair salons, clinics, car-repair shops, studios, gyms, consultants) who run bookings themselves, often from a phone between customers.
- Secondary: those shops' customers, booking a slot on mobile from the shop's public link.
- Platform super admins manage tenants, plans and audit logs.

## Product Purpose
SYSRENT Booking gives a shop its own online booking page within minutes, without building a website. Success: customers book 24/7 without double-bookings, and the shop runs its daily queue (confirm, check-in, reschedule, cancel, no-show) in one place.

## Positioning
One booking system for any service business: availability computed from working hours, leave, holidays and buffers, with database-level slot locking so two customers can never take the same slot.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Multi-tenant (Supabase RLS); each shop has slug URL / subdomain / custom domain.
- Multi-branch, multi-staff, per-branch timezone; customer records; plan quotas.
- Each shop sets its own `primary_color`; the public booking pages and its dashboard must keep rendering in that colour.
- Interface language: Thai.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: SYSRENT Booking (SYSRENT.SHOP).
- Existing marketing and UI copy is kept as written.

## Evidence on Hand
No testimonials, customer logos, metrics or screenshots exist. Do not fabricate them.

## Product Principles
- The shop's brand comes first on its own pages; SYSRENT stays in the background.
- Built for a busy owner on a phone: fast to scan, nothing to learn.
- Trust through correctness: what's shown as free is really free.
